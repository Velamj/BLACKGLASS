Benchmark: the 1993 PC game as specified by the owner's master brief. Initial reference classifications follow that brief; detailed historical tuning still needs primary-source reference play. NEW marks additions requested for this successor. No original game asset or source is reused.

**Milestone 1 foundation passes its bounded gameplay, save/load, Windows packaging and captured-scene performance checks.** Archive04 packaged successfully for Windows through UAT in 408.19 seconds (exit 0) and ran through ordinary mouse/keyboard controls: four operatives entered the facility, acquired and escorted the specialist, and all four survivors extracted for 6000 credits once. Quicksave/load preserved the won state without another reward; abort, failure and restart were exercised. All four operatives also boarded the van, traveled along the western street, stopped and exited. These are real packaged-game observations, not source inspection.

Run07 passed the expanded native automation scenario in 116.165077 seconds: test result Success, game process and runner exit 0, zero errors and zero failed tests. Four default operatives actually walked into four distinct van seats and exited, including the height-sensitive boundary-approach regression. Its isolated background AI does not prove natural behavior or all full-game acceptance tests. Commuters and patrols were observed during ordinary Archive04 play, while panic, escalation and balance still need verification. Archive05 packaged successfully through UAT in 64.54 seconds (exit 0), with the cooked original material, both textures and map verified. Its actual 1920x1080 game shows the darker industrial palette, surface/façade details, coat silhouettes, HUD and minimap. From a fresh start, ordinary Space/E input walked all four operatives into the van; a western-street trip, X stop and E exits worked. Archive05 loaded Archive04's completed-mission save, retained 6000 credits through F5/F9, and restarted through F8. That loaded win is not a fresh Archive05 mission completion. Real captures: [district](Evidence/package05-district.png) and [completed-mission save loaded](Evidence/package05-won-save-load.png). The formal Archive05 1920x1080 benchmark passed with game and runner exit 0: mean 11.306563 ms (about 88.44 FPS), p95 12.1444 ms. This measures the current foundation scene; production art and larger missions need fresh benchmarks. Core C++ contracts remain standalone fixtures; native engine world state is authoritative. Procedural foundation art and articulated code animation remain placeholders; neural and campaign systems are not complete.
